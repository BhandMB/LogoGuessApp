# Logo Guess Content Rights Review

## Asset inventory
Maintain a record of each logo or brand image, its source, permitted use, license or terms, and review date.

Publicly viewable images are not automatically free to redistribute. Prefer assets with clear permission or suitable licenses, and retain attribution where required.

## Review process
Check newly added assets before release, remove assets with unclear provenance, and document replacement work.

Every shipped image should have a traceable source and an approved basis for use. This checklist is not legal advice.
