# LogoGuess Content and Attribution Policy

## Content requirements
Every logo asset used in the game should have a recorded source, permitted-use basis, attribution requirement, and review date. Do not assume an image is reusable merely because it is publicly accessible.

## Asset metadata
Maintain a content record with asset identifier, brand or subject label, source URL, license or permission notes, required credit, and the game version in which it appears.

## Product safeguards
- Avoid implying sponsorship or endorsement by a brand.
- Provide required attribution in an accessible credits area.
- Remove or replace assets when permission is unclear or a rights holder requests review.
- Keep user-submitted assets separate from the curated question bank.

## Release review
Confirm that every shipped asset has a completed metadata record and that attribution is visible where required. Escalate uncertain rights questions for legal review rather than treating this checklist as legal advice.
