# LogoGuess Content Attribution and Review

Date: 2026-11-20

## Purpose

Maintain a review record for visual assets and clues used in the logo quiz.

## Asset register

For each asset, record its source, license or permission basis, allowed uses, attribution text if required, retrieval date, and any restrictions on modification or redistribution. Do not assume that an image being publicly viewable makes it free to reuse.

## Editorial review

Check that each question has one defensible answer, clue wording is unambiguous, spelling is correct, and difficulty is appropriate for the intended round. Avoid misleading or outdated brand descriptions.

## Release checklist

- [ ] Asset permission or license is documented.
- [ ] Required attribution is displayed where applicable.
- [ ] Question and answer pair has been reviewed.
- [ ] Duplicate or near-duplicate questions are checked.
- [ ] Removed assets are also removed from packaged builds.

## Scope

This is a content governance checklist, not a legal opinion or proof of asset clearance.
