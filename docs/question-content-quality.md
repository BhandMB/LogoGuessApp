# Question Content Quality

New logo questions should be reviewed for clarity, answerability, and consistent difficulty.

## Review checklist
- The image or clue is recognizable at the target display size.
- The accepted answer is unambiguous and normalized consistently.
- Distractors are plausible but not misleading.
- The question does not depend on inaccessible color-only information.
- Difficulty is tagged using a documented rule rather than intuition alone.

## Regression pass
Check question rendering, answer comparison, feedback text, score updates, and progression to the next round.
